Features

Everything you need

Designed to feel as satisfying as a real spinning wheel, with a few superpowers.

🎡

3D Chrome Wheel

A premium chrome-bezel wheel with physics-based spin, haptic pointer ticks, and a satisfying settle animation as it lands.

✏️

Fully Customisable

Type anything — dinner options, team names, yes/no decisions. The wheel instantly adapts to however many entries you add.

👆

Tap or Flick

Tap for a full spin or drag and release to flick the wheel with your own momentum. Fast flick = long spin. Your choice.

🎉

Winner Celebration

The winning slice glows white, haptics fire, and confetti flies. Removing the winner and spinning again is one tap away.

💾

Options Persist

Your wheel is waiting every time you open the app. Options are saved on-device so you never have to retype them.

Deep navy backgrounds, glass-effect cards, and premium typography. QuikPick looks great day or night.

FAQ

How do I add my own options?

Tap the Options button at the bottom of the wheel screen, type your entry, and press the + button or Return.

Will my options be saved?

Yes — options are stored on your device and persist between sessions.

Can I reset to Yes / No?

In the Options sheet, tap Use Default Yes / No at the bottom.

Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

QuikPick is built with your privacy in mind. This policy explains what we collect and how it's used — the short answer is: almost nothing, and it stays on your device.

Information We Collect

QuikPick does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers. All data you enter — your custom wheel options — is stored exclusively on your device using Apple's standard UserDefaults system.

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Your wheel option text labels (e.g. "Pizza", "Sushi")

This data never leaves your device. It is not shared with us, third parties, or any analytics service.

Analytics & Tracking

QuikPick contains no analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or third-party tracking of any kind. We do not know how often you open the app, what options you create, or anything else about your usage.

Children's Privacy

QuikPick is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing to protect beyond what Apple's platform already provides.

Third-Party Services

QuikPick does not integrate any third-party SDKs or services. The app uses only Apple's first-party frameworks.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will post the revised version here with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
